# Accuracy report

The site is roughly accurate. The counts, the feeling and root splits, and the exclusivity rules hold. A student would learn something wrong in the places below.

Checked against Bhikkhu Bodhi’s *Comprehensive Manual of Abhidhamma*, Nārada’s *Manual of Abhidhamma*, and AN 5.177.

## Wrong

**Right livelihood names the wrong trades.** `cetasika.md`, the right-livelihood study page, and the three-abstinences mix-up all list weapons, poisons, intoxicants, living beings, or deceit. AN 5.177, which Bodhi and Nārada are summarizing, is weapons, living beings, meat, intoxicants, and poison. Meat is missing. Deceit belongs to a different list in MN 117 (scheming, persuading, hinting, belittling, pursuing gain with gain). The abstention rule itself is right: one at a time in ordinary wholesome consciousness, all three fixed together in supramundane consciousness.

## Misleading

**The three abstinences are fixed in fruition as well as the path.** The exclusivity heading and the closing note in `sampayoga.md` say they become fixed only in the path. The right-speech entry calls this a permanent quality of path consciousness. Bodhi says they are present together in every supramundane consciousness, path and fruition. The sheet’s own supramundane block and its count of 16 already include both.

**The mind-door chain invents a stage called meaning.** `vithi.md` says the mind grasps the object as a whole, then its name, then its meaning. Ledi Sayadaw, quoted by Bodhi, has a conformational process, then the whole, then the color, then the entity, then the name. There is no meaning stage.

**Absorption is not seven ordinary javanas replaced by one absorption moment.** Bodhi: any of eight sense-sphere javanas accompanied by knowledge runs four times or three times as preparation, access, conformity, and change-of-lineage, and then absorption arises. A beginner’s absorption javana runs once. In a mastered attainment many absorption javanas occur. Path consciousness is one moment, then two or three fruition moments. The names in the sheet match. The “seven replaced” count does not.

**Death consciousness does not come straight after five javanas.** Bodhi: cuti arises at the end of a cognitive process or at the dissolution of the life-continuum. After the five weak javanas, two registration cittas may follow, and life-continuum may follow, before cuti. The five javanas, the three signs, and “cuti does not cause the next life” are right.

**The eightfold split of beautiful sense-sphere consciousness turns on wisdom alone.** `citta.md` says it turns on the abstinences, the illimitables, and wisdom. Those first two are occasional and do not generate the split. The table under that sentence is right.

**“As mine” is greed, not wrong view.** `citta.md` glosses wrong view as taking the object as a self, as permanent, and as mine. “Mine” is the greed in all eight greed-rooted cittas. The view that marks four of them is the justification.

**Remorse, as a cetasika, is always unwholesome.** `cetasika.md` says remorse that leads to correction is useful and the churning that follows is not. Nārada: in the Vinaya, *kukkucca* can mean healthy doubt about the rules, and that is commended. In the Abhidhamma it is repentance, and it is not commended.

**Sloth and torpor always arise together.** The mix-up essay says the sheet does not require that. Bodhi: they are optional in the five prompted unwholesome cittas, and when present they come as a pair. The study pages already treat them as a pair. The torpor entry’s “follows on sloth” is the same slip.

**Intention is not kamma in every moment.** The intention mix-up and the `kamma` term gloss identify intention with kamma. Cetanā is in all 89 cittas. Bodhi: kamma is volitional activity in wholesome or unwholesome cittas. The cetanā in a rootless sense consciousness does not make kamma. The homepage already puts kamma in the javanas.

## Minor

- Life-continuum vibration is called registering. Those two cittas only vibrate. The object is first taken at five-door adverting. Registration is the later tadārammaṇa.
- Five-door adverting is said to choose which sense consciousness follows. It turns to the object. Which consciousness follows is fixed by which organ was struck.
- “Only seven moments make kamma” leaves out an Arahant’s functional javanas, which produce none.
- Equanimity is “by far” the most common feeling. Inside the 89 it is 47 against 38 joyful. On the 121 reckoning, joy is the larger class, 62 against 55.
- The term `sukha` treats bodily pleasure and the jhāna factor as one thing. Bodhi says the jhāna factor is mental joy. The feeling-and-rapture essay already states that split.
- Attention is said to select the object that contact then meets. The seven universals arise and cease together.
- The path is called kamma whose result cannot be delayed. The immediacy matches both manuals. Nārada adds that the four supramundane wholesome cittas are not treated as kamma. Bodhi does not, and the sheet follows Bodhi.

## What checked out

The 89 and 121 totals, every plane and root split, and the homepage sentence about them. All 52 factor names, groups, and standard functions aside from the livelihood list. Every association total in `sampayoga.md`. The exclusivity rules for greed and hatred, view and conceit, envy, stinginess and worry, doubt and determination, and compassion and sympathetic joy. The five-door order, the four object-grades, the mind-door having no separate determining moment, and kamma being made in the javanas and settled at determining. All 52 study-page ranges. The homepage, the group labels, and the rest of the mix-up essays.
